I am currently a master's student at the Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences (CASIA), under the supervision of [Prof. Kai Li](https://kli-casia.github.io/). I completed my undergraduate studies at the College of Computer Science and Electronic Engineering, Hunan University.

My research interests lie in the areas of Agent Harness, AI Agent, and Agentic RL, with a particular focus on how agent harnesses and reinforcement learning techniques can be used to build and improve AI agents.
